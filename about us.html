UNDERSTAND MORE ABOUT SOWETO PEFA CATHEDRAL.

Soweto Pefa Cathedral is one of the leading pentecostal movement in Nairobi based in Embakasi East Constituency, Nairobi at Lower Savannah, within Soweto area. Headed by Bishop John Mwaka Musyoki as v ision bearer. The church does community outreach through cleaning the area as they preach gospel and win souls to the kingdom. The church believes that everybody is somebody slogan which has attracted thousands of congregants for a felowship as it epitomizes the true life of the person of Jesus Christ.
